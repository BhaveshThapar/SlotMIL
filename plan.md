# SlotMIL: Slot Attention as an Interpretable MIL Pooling Bottleneck for Volumetric CT — Implementation-Ready Research Dossier

## TL;DR
- **The precise idea is narrowly novel but under threat.** Locatello-style iterative slot attention (softmax-over-slots + GRU) as the pooling replacement in ABMIL/CLAM/TransMIL, applied to volumetric CT with slots specializing into radiological findings, was NOT found in the literature — BUT two papers sit dangerously close: **Slot-MIL** (arXiv:2311.17466, slot attention as MIL pooling for pathology WSI) and **INSIGHT** (arXiv:2412.02012, interpretable weakly-supervised MIL aggregator evaluated on CT+WSI, non-slot). Both must be cited and differentiated or the paper is dead on arrival.
- **The winning framing is "interpretable, localization-capable MIL pooling for CT, evaluated against lesion masks you already have."** The backbone of the paper is datasets carrying BOTH weak bag labels for training AND pixel/instance masks for localization evaluation: LIDC-IDRI (4-radiologist nodule masks), MosMedData (50 GGO/consolidation masks), plus MedMNIST3D for fast sanity checks. This lets you measure slot-to-finding alignment quantitatively — the one experiment reviewers cannot get from Slot-MIL or INSIGHT.
- **Feasibility is high on your compute** because of one trick: precompute and cache frozen DINOv2/DenseNet features once, then train only the tiny slot module. This turns the whole project into single-24GB-GPU-scale work and makes the 12-week ISBI timeline realistic.

## Key Findings

1. **MIL is overwhelmingly WSI-centric.** The dominant lineage — ABMIL/Gated-Attention (Ilse et al., ICML 2018), CLAM (Lu et al., Nature BME 2021), DSMIL (CVPR 2021), TransMIL (NeurIPS 2021), DTFD-MIL (CVPR 2022), and 2024-2025 successors (ACMIL/ECCV 2024, MambaMIL/MICCAI 2024, RRT-MIL, GMMamba/ICCV 2025) — is almost entirely developed and benchmarked on pathology whole-slide images. MIL-for-CT exists but is a much smaller, more fragmented literature, and slot-based pooling for CT is unclaimed.

2. **Slot Attention now works on real images but is finicky.** DINOSAUR (Seitzer et al., ICLR 2023) was the breakthrough that scaled slots to COCO/PASCAL via frozen-feature reconstruction. Known failure modes are well documented: slot collapse/duplication, sensitivity to the number of slots K, and training instability. The literature offers concrete fixes — implicit differentiation (Chang et al., NeurIPS 2022), learnable-query init + bi-level optimization (BO-QSA, ICLR 2023).

3. **The intersection is nearly empty but not empty.** Slot attention as MIL pooling exists for pathology (Slot-MIL) and interpretable MIL aggregation exists for CT (INSIGHT, non-slot). The specific combination — iterative GRU slot attention as MIL pooling on 3D CT with clinically-named slots evaluated against lesion masks — appears genuinely unclaimed.

4. **Prototype methods are the reviewer's default comparison.** ProtoMIL (Rymarczyk et al., 2021; and a MICCAI 2025 sparse-autoencoder ProtoMIL) and Concept Bottleneck Models (Koh et al., ICML 2020) are the interpretable-by-design baselines a reviewer will demand. The crisp distinction: prototypes are fixed, global, learned templates; slots are input-dependent, competitive latents recomputed per bag.

5. **You have the data.** LIDC-IDRI + MosMedData + MedMNIST3D + your existing COV19D pipeline cover the whole experimental design with public data and no new IRB.

## Details

### PART 1 — WHAT IS ALREADY DONE (literature map)

**(a) MIL for medical imaging — the lineage.**
- **Attention-based Deep MIL / Gated-Attention MIL (Ilse, Tomczak, Welling, ICML 2018, arXiv:1802.04712).** The foundational learnable pooling: bag embedding z = Σ aᵢhᵢ with attention weights aᵢ = softmax(wᵀ tanh(V hᵢ)); the gated variant multiplies in a sigmoid gate tanh(Vh)⊙σ(Uh). Interpretability claim: attention weights indicate key instances/ROIs. Datasets: MNIST-bags, two histopathology sets (breast, colon). Official PyTorch: github.com/AMLab-Amsterdam/AttentionDeepMIL. This is the team's existing model family (seven Gated-Attention MIL models in the CVPR 2026 workshop paper). Single scalar weight per instance → single pooled vector.
- **CLAM (Lu et al., Nature Biomedical Engineering 2021; arXiv:2004.09666).** Attention pooling + instance-level clustering constraint for weakly-supervised WSI classification; single-branch (CLAM-SB) and multi-branch (CLAM-MB) variants. Interpretability via attention heatmaps. Code: github.com/mahmoodlab/CLAM. WSI only.
- **DSMIL (Li, Li, Eliceiri, CVPR 2021).** Dual-stream: a max-pooling instance stream identifies a critical instance, a second stream computes attention of all instances against it (non-local). Multi-scale WSI. WSI only.
- **TransMIL (Shao et al., NeurIPS 2021).** Self-attention over instances (correlated MIL) with the Nyström approximation for efficiency + a "PPEG" positional module. Code: github.com/szc19990412/TransMIL. WSI only.
- **DTFD-MIL (Zhang et al., CVPR 2022).** Double-tier feature distillation using pseudo-bags to expand effective bag count for small cohorts. Code: github.com/hrzhang1123/DTFD-MIL (+ unofficial Dootmaan/DTFD-MIL.PyTorch). WSI only.
- **2023-2025 successors, all WSI:** ACMIL (Attention-Challenging MIL, ECCV 2024, github.com/dazhangyu123/ACMIL) adds attention-entropy maximization (AEM) to fight attention concentration/overfitting; MambaMIL (MICCAI 2024, github.com/isyangshu/MambaMIL) uses a selective state-space model with sequence reordering (SR-Mamba) for long instance sequences; RRT-MIL and GMMamba (ICCV 2025) continue the efficiency/interaction line; IBMIL (CVPR 2023 highlight, github.com/HHHedo/IBMIL) does interventional de-confounding. Mahmood lab's MIL-Lab (ICML 2025, github.com/mahmoodlab/MIL-Lab) packages ABMIL/CLAM/TransMIL/DSMIL/DTFD/RRT/ILRA under one API — a useful baseline harness.
- **MIL-for-CT (the sparse part):** exists but scattered — hierarchical MIL for COVID-19 severity in 3D CT (M²UNet, arXiv:2005.03832); LIDC nodule MIL (Univ. Louisville thesis 2021); HLFSRNN-MIL (Applied Sciences 2024, 3D CT lung cancer + pneumonia); ATMIL for multiple myeloma (arXiv:2107.07805). There is no established slot-based MIL for CT. Quantitatively, MIL-for-CT is roughly an order of magnitude smaller than the WSI MIL literature and lacks a canonical benchmark harness the way CLAM/MIL-Lab serve WSI.

**(b) Slot Attention lineage.**
- **Slot Attention (Locatello et al., NeurIPS 2020).** K slots initialized from a learnable Gaussian compete for input features via attention normalized OVER SLOTS (softmax across slots, so slots compete to "explain" each input token), then a GRU + MLP residual update over T iterations. Permutation-invariant, variable-slot-count capable.
- **DINOSAUR (Seitzer et al., ICLR 2023, arXiv:2209.14860).** The pivotal result: reconstruct frozen self-supervised ViT (DINO) features instead of pixels → object discovery on real COCO/VOC. Uses vanilla slot attention; recommends an MLP decoder as first choice. Still reports training instability. Official code: github.com/amazon-science/object-centric-learning-framework; VideoSAUR (NeurIPS 2023) extends to video.
- **Stability/quality successors:** Implicit Slot Attention / "Object Representations as Fixed Points" (Chang et al., NeurIPS 2022, arXiv:2207.00787) — treat slots as a fixed point, backprop through one step with implicit gradients; one line of code; O(1) backward memory; stabilizes the Jacobian and removes the need for gradient clipping / LR warmup / tuning iteration count. BO-QSA (Jia et al., ICLR 2023, arXiv:2210.08990) — learnable query init + bi-level optimization with straight-through estimator; SOTA on 3 synthetic + 7 real datasets; code github.com/YuLiu-LY/BO-QSA (and github.com/Wall-Facer-liuyu/BO-QSA). SLATE (transformer decoder), Slot Diffusion / LSD (latent-diffusion decoder), Invariant Slot Attention (ICML 2023, slot-centric reference frames), Slot-VPS (CVPR 2022, video panoptic), SlotFormer (dynamics). Spotlight Attention (spatial locality prior) and covariance-regularization work address the bottleneck.
- **Known failure modes (well documented):** slot collapse (all slots identical), slot duplication (two slots bind one object), high sensitivity to K (an entire paper — arXiv:2305.18890, "Sensitivity of Slot-Based Object-Centric Models to their Number of Slots" — studies this), and unstable early training with random init. Important nuance: that paper found random init can OUTPERFORM learnable init in some purely-unsupervised settings — so BO-QSA's learnable queries are not a guaranteed win and must be ablated.

**(c) THE INTERSECTION (adversarial near-miss report).**
- **Slot-MIL — "Slot-Mixup with Subsampling: A Simple Regularization for WSI Classification" (Keum et al., arXiv:2311.17466). THE DIRECT THREAT.** Explicitly proposes "a slot-attention based MIL method which aggregates patches into a fixed number of slots" and calls it a "computationally efficient pooling-based model for WSI classification." What it did: slot pooling of patches (citing Locatello + Set Transformer inducing points), plus a subsampling+mixup regularizer on slots; pathology WSI (Camelyon/TCGA-style) only. What it did NOT do: no CT/volumetric data; no framing of slots as interpretable radiological findings; leans on PMA/inducing-point-style pooling rather than the full iterative softmax-over-slots + GRU update; no slot-to-lesion-mask localization evaluation. This is the closest prior art and must be cited as such.
- **INSIGHT — "Explainable Weakly-Supervised Medical Image Analysis" (Zhang, Chen, Kanan, arXiv:2412.02012; MLHC 2025). SECOND-CLOSEST.** A weakly-supervised MIL aggregator with built-in heatmaps, evaluated on BOTH CT and WSI, claiming SOTA classification + weakly-labeled segmentation. But it does NOT use slot attention — it uses detection + context conv modules fused into internal heatmaps. It occupies your exact application niche (interpretable CT MIL aggregation) with a different mechanism. Cite and differentiate on mechanism (competitive iterative slots vs conv heatmaps) and per-finding slot specialization. Project page: zhangdylan83.github.io/ewsmia.
- **Future Slot Prediction for Unsupervised Object Discovery in Surgical Video (Liao et al., MICCAI 2025, arXiv:2507.01882; code github.com/PCASOlab/Xslot).** Slot attention in medicine, but surgical VIDEO object discovery (dynamic temporal slot transformer, DTST), self-supervised, not MIL classification and not CT volumes. Same group: Slot-BERT (Medical Image Analysis 2026), FORLA (NeurIPS 2025). Establishes slot attention "works" on real medical data — helpful precedent, not a novelty threat.
- **Slot attention as pooling in other domains** (reduces novelty of the generic idea, not the CT specialization): Set Transformer's Pooling-by-Multihead-Attention (Lee et al., 2019) with k learnable seed vectors is the conceptual ancestor of "K latents pool a set"; ILRA-MIL uses learnable inducing points to aggregate patches; PICASO (arXiv:2107.08305) relates slot competition to soft K-means. Frame SlotMIL's novelty as the *iterative competitive GRU slot mechanism + CT specialization + mask-validated interpretability*, not "latents pool a set," which is old.

**(d) Adjacent interpretable-by-design approaches.**
- **Concept Bottleneck Models (Koh et al., ICML 2020).** Predict human-defined concepts, then predict label from concepts; supports test-time intervention. Medical variants abound (post-hoc CBM, label-free CBM with LLM concepts, MVP-CBM, causal CBM for chest X-ray). Weakness: needs concept annotations (or noisy LLM-generated ones). SlotMIL differs: no predefined concept labels; slots emerge from competition and are aligned to findings post hoc via masks.
- **Prototype networks: ProtoPNet/ProtoTree/XProtoNet, and ProtoMIL (Rymarczyk et al., 2021, arXiv:2108.10612) + a MICCAI 2025 ProtoMIL (sparse-autoencoder concepts, github.com/ss-sun/ProtoMIL).** ProtoMIL does "case-based reasoning on visual prototypes" for WSI. **The crisp distinction a reviewer will demand:** prototypes are FIXED, GLOBAL vectors learned once and shared across all inputs (nearest-neighbor to training patches); slots are INPUT-DEPENDENT, COMPETITIVE latents re-initialized and re-solved for every bag via iterative attention. Prototypes answer "which training exemplar does this resemble"; slots answer "which coherent groups of instances exist in THIS scan and which drove the prediction." Make this explicit and cite both ProtoMIL papers.

### PART 2 — NOVELTY CLAIM AND RISKS

**Defensible novelty claim (one sentence):** *We introduce SlotMIL, the first use of iterative competitive slot attention (softmax-over-slots + GRU refinement) as the pooling bottleneck in attention-based MIL for volumetric CT, yielding a set of K input-dependent slot representations whose attention masks provide built-in, mask-validated lesion localization from weak volume-level labels alone — evaluated for slot-to-radiological-finding alignment against public lesion annotations (LIDC-IDRI, MosMedData).* The narrower, safer secondary claim, if accuracy is neutral: *slot attention is an accuracy-neutral, interpretability-enhancing drop-in pooling layer for CT MIL, with the first quantitative slot-to-finding alignment study in radiology.*

**Top 5 reviewer objections and the experiment that kills each:**
1. *"Slots are just multi-head attention / multi-head ABMIL."* Kill it with a **multi-head ABMIL baseline matched in parameter count and head count = K** (no iterative competition, no GRU, no softmax-over-slots). If SlotMIL's slots specialize (higher slot-to-mask purity, lower redundancy) while multi-head ABMIL heads are redundant/duplicative, the competitive mechanism — not extra capacity — is doing the work. Report per-head vs per-slot NMI against lesion masks.
2. *"Why not just ProtoMIL?"* Run ProtoMIL as a baseline; argue and empirically show input-dependent competitive slots vs fixed global prototypes (Part 1d), and test intervention/robustness. Show slots adapt to per-scan lesion configurations that a fixed prototype set cannot.
3. *"Slot assignments aren't clinically meaningful; you post-hoc named them."* Kill it with **quantitative slot-to-finding alignment measured against public masks** (LIDC nodules, MosMed GGO/consolidation): slot-mask Dice/IoU, slot purity/NMI, and a Hungarian slot→finding assignment computed on a held-out split, so naming is fixed before test. Add a permutation/consistency test: the same slot index binds the same finding across bags at rate ≫ chance.
4. *"Does it beat gated-attention MIL on accuracy?"* Report AUC/macro-F1/balanced accuracy vs the team's Gated-Attention MIL and full baseline suite with seeds + significance tests. If it wins, great; if neutral, pivot to the interpretability claim (which the baselines cannot match) — pre-committed fallback.
5. *"K was tuned on test."* Kill it with a **K ablation on validation only**, report test at the val-selected K, and show a sensitivity curve. Cite the K-sensitivity literature and report variance across seeds for each K.

### PART 4 — METHOD DESIGN

**Feature extraction (recommend DINOv2 ViT-B, frozen, per-slice 2D features, cached).** Rationale: the team already has DINOv2 and RadImageNet-DenseNet-121 pipelines; DINOSAUR shows frozen ViT features are exactly what make slots discover objects on real images. Default = DINOv2 ViT-B/14 patch-token features per axial slice (keeps spatial grid for localization). Ablate: DINOv2 ViT-S/L, RadImageNet DenseNet-121, a CT foundation model (CT-CLIP vision encoder or Merlin, if weights load cleanly), and a light 3D CNN. Keep backbones frozen for the ISBI version (feature caching); finetuning is a MICCAI-phase ablation.

**Bag construction.** Default = 2D-slice-as-instance (each axial slice → one instance embedding, preserving the patch-token grid so slot attention masks map back to voxels). 2.5D (adjacent-slice stacks) and 3D-patch-as-instance are ablations. Handle variable slice counts by using slot attention's native set-invariance (no fixed N); for very long volumes (RSNA PE up to ~1,000 slices) subsample slices per epoch (stochastic) and use all at inference. Bag size interacts with K: too few slices per finding starves slots; report bag-size ablation.

**Slot Attention module (the actual algorithm).** Inputs = instance embeddings H ∈ ℝ^{N×D_in}, projected to D. Initialize K slots (default: learnable-query init per BO-QSA; ablate random-Gaussian). For t = 1..T (default T=3):
1. slots_prev = slots; slots = LayerNorm(slots)
2. attn logits M = (1/√D) · q(slots) · k(H)ᵀ ∈ ℝ^{K×N}
3. **attn = softmax(M, axis=slots)** — the competition (each input token's weight distributed across slots)
4. weights W = attn / attn.sum(axis=N, keepdim) (weighted-mean normalization)
5. updates U = W · v(H) ∈ ℝ^{K×D}
6. slots = GRU(state=slots_prev, input=U)
7. slots = slots + MLP(LayerNorm(slots)) (residual)
Use **implicit differentiation** (Chang et al. 2022): run T iters without grad, then one more update step with grad (one-line change) → O(1) backward memory, stable Jacobian, no need for gradient clipping/warmup. This is the single most important stability trick; adopt by default.

**Readout head (preserve "which slot drove the prediction").** Recommend **per-slot logits + learned attention-over-slots aggregation** with a softmax gate g_k, so bag logit = Σ_k g_k · classifier(slot_k), and g_k directly reports which slot drove the positive call. Compare against (i) max over slot logits (most interpretable, "at least one slot is positive" = classic MIL assumption), (ii) a small transformer over slots (most expressive, least interpretable), (iii) concat+MLP (baseline). Report the interpretability-accuracy tradeoff across readouts.

**Losses/regularizers.** Primary = bag-level BCE (multi-label: RAD-ChestCT/CT-RATE) or CE (COV19D/MedMNIST). Add a **slot-diversity/orthogonality penalty** (penalize off-diagonal slot-slot cosine similarity) to fight collapse/duplication — ablate on/off. Optional **DINOSAUR-style auxiliary feature-reconstruction loss**: the hypothesis is it is largely UNNECESSARY when a supervised bag label is present (the label supervises specialization); test explicitly (with/without recon) — a clean, publishable ablation either way. Consider an entropy regularizer on slot-attention masks (spatial compactness).

**Failure modes & mitigations (pre-committed):** collapse → diversity loss + implicit diff + learnable queries; duplication → increase K margin, orthogonality penalty; K-sensitivity → validation-only K sweep + report curve; early instability → implicit diff + LR warmup; dead slots → monitor per-slot mask mass, report count of active slots.

**Localization extraction.** Slot attention masks (attn ∈ ℝ^{K×N}) map each slot to its instance/patch weights; reshape to the ViT patch grid per slice, bilinearly upsample to voxel resolution → per-slot 3D heatmap. Threshold for Dice/IoU vs lesion masks; visualize as slot-colored overlays.

**Baselines that MUST run** (public code in parentheses): mean pooling, max pooling, ABMIL + Gated-Attention MIL (AMLab-Amsterdam/AttentionDeepMIL; the team's existing model), TransMIL (szc19990412/TransMIL), DSMIL, CLAM-SB/MB (mahmoodlab/CLAM), DTFD-MIL (hrzhang1123/DTFD-MIL), **multi-head ABMIL with matched params (objection #1)**, and ProtoMIL (ss-sun/ProtoMIL or the Rymarczyk repo). **Metrics.** Classification: AUC, macro-F1 (team reported 0.928 macro-F1 previously — use as internal bar), balanced accuracy. Localization: Dice/IoU vs masks, pointing-game accuracy, instance-level AUC on LIDC nodules. Interpretability: slot-to-finding alignment (Hungarian assignment), slot purity/NMI vs annotations, faithfulness via deletion/insertion curves. Stability: variance across ≥5 seeds, slot-permutation consistency across bags.

**Ablation grid:** K ∈ {2,4,6,8,12}; T ∈ {1,3,5,7}; backbone (DINOv2-S/B/L, DenseNet-121, CT-FM); frozen vs finetuned; diversity reg on/off; recon loss on/off; bag size / slice sampling; readout head type; init (random vs learnable query); vanilla vs implicit-diff.

**Statistical rigor.** ≥5 seeds; 5-fold CV or the dataset's official split (MedMNIST3D and COV19D have fixed splits); paired significance testing (DeLong for AUC; bootstrap CIs) — standard for ISBI/MICCAI. Report mean±std and significance vs Gated-Attention MIL.

**Cross-domain.** Use COV19D's multi-source structure and RAD-ChestCT↔CT-RATE (shared ~16 labels) for cross-center generalization — directly extends the team's domain-shift expertise.

**Compute estimate & the key trick.** **Precompute frozen DINOv2 features once, cache to disk, then train only the slot module.** Storage: DINOv2 ViT-B gives ~256 patch tokens × 768 dims × 2 bytes (fp16) ≈ 0.4 MB/slice at full grid, or a few KB/slice for pooled CLS. For LIDC (~1,000 scans × ~200 slices) full-grid patch tokens ≈ low-hundreds of GB (cut ~4-10× by using fp16 + only in-lung slices; pooled features ≈ a few GB). Once cached, the slot module is tiny (a few M params): a full MIL run over cached features is minutes-to-low-hours on a single 24GB card. The full ablation grid (dozens of runs × 5 seeds) fits comfortably in low-hundreds of A100/A6000 GPU-hours; feature extraction is the one-time cost (roughly a single GPU-day for LIDC/MosMed). This is what makes the project single-GPU-feasible and the timeline realistic.

### PART 7 — RISK REGISTER & FALLBACKS

- **Slots collapse / never specialize.** Fallback: a *negative-results + analysis* paper on why supervised bag labels alone don't induce slot specialization in CT, with the diversity/recon-loss ablations as the contribution. Publishable at MIDL or an ISBI/MICCAI workshop.
- **Slots specialize but not into clinically meaningful groups.** Fallback: report slot-to-finding NMI honestly; reframe as "slots capture data-driven substructure ≠ radiological taxonomy," a cautionary interpretability study. MIDL-appropriate.
- **SlotMIL underperforms Gated-Attention MIL on accuracy.** Fallback (pre-committed): "accuracy-neutral interpretable pooling layer" — lead with localization/interpretability where baselines can't compete. Still ISBI-viable.
- **Localization shows slots attend to spurious regions.** Fallback: deletion/insertion faithfulness + spurious-correlation analysis becomes the contribution (ties to IBMIL-style de-confounding); pair with a fix attempt (mask-guided regularizer, à la RAD-ChestCT mask loss).
- **Deadline arrives with incomplete results.** Fallback: submit MedMNIST3D + LIDC-only results as a 4-page ISBI "early results/method" paper; defer the full study to MICCAI. The frozen-feature-cache design specifically de-risks this by making core experiments fast.

## Recommendations
1. **Lock the framing now to the one experiment competitors lack: quantitative slot-to-radiological-finding alignment against public lesion masks (LIDC + MosMed).** This is the defensible moat given Slot-MIL and INSIGHT already exist. Do NOT sell "slot attention as MIL pooling" as the novelty (Slot-MIL took it) or "interpretable CT MIL aggregator" (INSIGHT took it) — sell the mask-validated, competitive-iterative-slot, CT-specialized combination.

3. **Run the multi-head-ABMIL matched-parameter control early (Week 4-5).** If SlotMIL's advantage over it is not visible by Week 7, invoke the accuracy-neutral-interpretability fallback and reallocate to localization depth.
4. **Cite Slot-MIL, INSIGHT, ProtoMIL (both), CBM, and the Liao surgical-slot work explicitly in Related Work with a differentiation sentence each.** Reviewers will find them; pre-empt.
5. **Staged go/no-go:** proceed to the MICCAI full version only if (a) slot-mask alignment ≫ chance and ≫ multi-head ABMIL, AND (b) classification is at least neutral vs Gated-Attention MIL. If only (a) holds, MICCAI as an interpretability paper; if neither, MIDL negative-results paper.
